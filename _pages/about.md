Hi! I'm a fifth year PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, affiliated with the [Center for Language and Speech Processing](https://www.clsp.jhu.edu){:target="_blank"} and the [Human Language Technology Center of Excellence](https://hltcoe.jhu.edu){:target="_blank"}. I am advised by [Benjamin Van Durme](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~vandurme/){:target="_blank"} and [Nicholas Andrews](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~noa/){:target="_blank"}.

My research in Natural Language Processing is interested in controlling generation from language models; recently I've been particularly interested in investigating control for fine-grained attributes (e.g. writing in the style of Aleem).

Previously I worked on developing robust [user representations](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.415/){:target="_blank"} for author verification and retrieval.

When I'm *not* at my desk, you can find me running, kickboxing, or playing softball.
